Append-only logical ledger.

FATTO / INTERPRETAZIONE / IPOTESI / DECISIONE / TEST / ESITO.

Required fields:
record_id, idea_id, hypothesis_id, evidence_id, root_evidence_id, source, timestamp, content_hash, causal_origin, independence_status, test, expected_result, actual_result, falsification_status, decision, actor, version.

P5 — Repetition from the same causal origin is not independent evidence.
P6 — Important claims remain traceable and falsifiable.
