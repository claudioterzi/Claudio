Start: **22 September 2026, 09:14:09 Europe/Rome**

This is the forward-only performance experiment behind the future **Il Pozzo di Raffaello** result charts.

It moves **no real money**.

## Starting notional

€500 virtual.

Initial paper allocation:

- 45% BTC
- 25% ETH
- 10% SOL
- 20% EUR cash

The initial EUR spot marks were taken from CoinGecko at activation:
BTC €74,452.10; ETH €2,381.92; SOL €101.78.

The experiment charges 0.20% simulated trading fee + 0.10% simulated slippage on traded notional. Therefore the first post-execution NAV is **€498.65**, not €500. This prevents the performance chart from pretending transaction friction does not exist.

## Why no tactical trade today

The adaptive policy was preregistered only after the starting prices were observed. To avoid choosing a trade using the same observation that defined the policy, the first tactical paper decision is forbidden until 24 hours after activation.

Hourly runs mark the portfolio only.

Daily runs may execute a virtual rebalance under the deterministic risk/momentum envelope in `R3_PAPER_FUND.yaml`.

Weekly runs compare the adaptive paper strategy against:
1. the same basket held passively;
2. BTC-only;
3. EUR cash.

## Truth rule for the website

The mockup percentages previously generated by image generation are **not historical results**.

From this activation onward, a result can be displayed only from preserved observations. A 1-month graph requires one actual month. A 2-month graph requires two actual months. Six-month and one-year graphs are not extrapolated.

Until then the site should say, for example:

> Storico forward verificabile: 11 giorni

not “1 mese” with invented data.

## What success means

Not “always profitable.”

The experiment asks whether a bounded adaptive policy can produce better **net** results or lower drawdown than its preregistered benchmarks while remaining reproducible and transparent.

Losses stay in the ledger.
