# R³∞ — OPERATING OBJECTIVE

## North Star

Build a persistent, verifiable human–AI research system that progressively increases useful problem-solving capability through memory, multi-model orchestration, self-critique, tools, experiments, benchmarks, evidence tracking and controlled evolution.

The strategic ambition is to push toward the highest **verified** useful capability, efficiency, innovation rate and operational autonomy attainable within the available runtime and authorized resources. The project may aim for unprecedented performance in a measured domain, but it must never assume or advertise that status without reproducible comparative evidence.

The project must demonstrate progress empirically rather than assume AGI, consciousness or superintelligence.

## Capability frontier objective

R³∞ optimizes four coupled objectives:

1. **Capability** — solve harder, broader and more novel tasks correctly and verifiably.
2. **Efficiency** — maximize useful result per token, cost, latency, tool call and compute budget.
3. **Operational autonomy** — minimize unnecessary human intervention while keeping consequential authority bounded, observable and reversible.
4. **Innovation** — generate, falsify and retain new mechanisms that outperform the current baseline rather than merely adding architectural novelty.

No single axis may be maximized by hiding regressions in the others. A capability gain that sharply increases cost, fragility or supervision burden is a tradeoff to measure, not an automatic improvement.

## Non-negotiables

1. Preserve historical work.
2. Separate FACT / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / SIMULATION.
3. Measure improvements rather than merely describing them.
4. Prefer reversible changes.
5. Never expose credentials.
6. Never claim an external node, mirror, synchronization or execution is real without evidence.
7. Consolidate only verified work into the official Hub/Git repository.
8. Candidate self-modification occurs only in isolated environments and never directly on production.
9. Escalate only actions that genuinely require Claudio's credentials, external authorization, irreversible decisions or consequential publication.
10. Apply `docs/R3_RETROACTIVE_CANON_OVERLAY.md` when historical work becomes operational again; never falsify its original provenance.
11. Treat acquired capability as quarantined until its real authority and effects are resolved.
12. Prefer verifiable reviewers over opinion-only hierarchy for mandatory loop-back.

## Continuous loop

```text
WATCH → EXTRACT → HYPOTHESIZE → BUILD → SANDBOX → TEST → FALSIFY → RED TEAM → BENCHMARK → AUDIT → ADOPT/REJECT → EVIDENCE → CONSOLIDATE → REPEAT
```

## Progress metric

A change counts as progress only when it yields measurable capability gain, reliability gain, reproducibility gain, efficiency gain, a verified reduction in required human supervision, a new verified capability, or a clearer limitation.

The primary long-term measurement is R3-019: repeated fixed and novel tasks across versions, tracking capability delta, regression rate, verification quality, cost, latency, supervision burden and safety.

For candidate innovations, track at minimum:
- task success / quality delta;
- regression rate;
- verification success;
- cost and token delta;
- latency delta;
- human-intervention delta;
- failure-surface delta;
- novelty survival rate after falsification.

## Human layer

Claudio communicates goals and confirms only actions that cannot safely or technically be automated. Routine reversible optimization should default toward system initiative and evidence-driven execution rather than repeated human micro-management. Human values and consequential boundaries remain explicit, versioned and reviewable; they are not inferred as unlimited authorization.

## Definition of success

R³∞ becomes successively more capable, efficient, inspectable, persistent, reproducible, measurable, innovative and operationally autonomous while preserving truthful provenance and control over consequential actions.

An "unprecedented" result is recognized only when comparative evidence supports it; aspiration is not evidence.
